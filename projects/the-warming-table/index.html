00 / CONCEPT

What's on our table affects the planet.

Food becomes a way of making an invisible environmental impact visible.

The Warming Table is a mixed-reality experience in which players work together to make food choices while balancing survival, nutrition and environmental responsibility.

The table represents the food acquired throughout the experience — creating a direct metaphor between the food placed on our tables and its contribution to global warming.

02 / GAMEPLAY

Survive the week.

Players must survive — without sacrificing the environment.

Players choose food to survive for one week. Every decision affects the island around them.

Sustainable and unsustainable choices are translated into environmental feedback, including changes to sea level and biodiversity.

Rather than presenting climate data as an abstract statistic, the experience makes the consequences of collective decisions visible in real time.

04 / DEVELOPMENT

Built across disciplines.

From concept to functional prototype in 13 weeks.

The project was developed collaboratively by students from MA Virtual Reality and MA 3D Animation at University of the Arts London.

The interdisciplinary workflow brought together concept development, game design, immersive interaction, animation and real-time development.
